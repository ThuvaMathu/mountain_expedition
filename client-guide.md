# Tamil Adventure - Admin Panel User Guide

Welcome to the Tamil Adventure Admin Panel! This guide provides a simple, step-by-step manual on how to manage your website content. 

---

## 🚀 The Golden Rule: How to Make Changes Live (Publishing)

Whenever you add, edit, or delete anything in the admin panel, the changes are saved to the database but **they do not appear on the live website immediately**. 

To make your changes visible to the public, you **must** follow this workflow:

1. **Save Your Changes:** First, create or edit your content in the specific section and click the **Save** or **Update** button on that page.
2. **Click Publish All:** Go to the left sidebar menu, scroll to the bottom, and click the **🚀 Publish All** button.
3. **Confirm:** A popup will appear. Click **Confirm**. 
4. **Wait:** The publishing process takes about **10 minutes**. Grab a coffee, and your live website will reflect all recent updates!

## 📊 Dashboard
The dashboard provides a quick overview.
* **View:** Click on "Dashboard" in the sidebar to view a snapshot of your site's overall activity.

---

## 📅 Bookings
Manage all customer bookings for tours and treks.
* **View Bookings:** Click on "Bookings". You will see a list of all recent customer reservations.
* **Manage:** You can view details, contact the customer, or update the booking status. *(Note: Typically, you do not create bookings from here; they arrive automatically when customers book through the public website).*

---

## 🚌 Tour & Travel
Manage your regular tourist packages.
* **Create a New Tour:** 
  1. Fill in the form at the top (Title, Description, Price, Image, etc.).
  2. Click **Add Package** (or the respective save button).
* **Edit an Existing Tour:** 
  1. Find the tour in the list below.
  2. Click the **Edit** button next to it.
  3. Change the details in the form.
  4. Click **Update**.
* **Delete a Tour:** 
  1. Find the tour in the list.
  2. Click **Delete** and confirm.

---

## ⛰️ Trekking
Manage your mountain and trekking adventures.
* **Create a New Trek:** 
  1. Fill out the details (Mountain Name, Difficulty, Duration, Itinerary, etc.).
  2. Click the **Save** button.
* **Edit a Trek:** 
  1. Scroll to your list of treks.
  2. Click **Edit**. The top form will populate with the trek's current info.
  3. Make your changes and click **Update**.
* **Delete a Trek:** 
  1. Click the **Delete** button next to the trek you want to remove.

---

## 📝 Blog Posts
Write and manage your articles.
* **Create a New Post:**
  1. Fill in the Title, Author, Tags, and choose a Date. Add a Main Image.
  2. Write your content in the text editor.
  3. Click **Publish Post**.
* **Edit a Post:**
  1. Find the post in the list below.
  2. Click **Edit**. 
  3. Update the text, title, or images.
  4. Click **Update Post**.
* **Delete a Post:**
  1. Next to the blog post, click **Delete**.

---

## 🖼️ Gallery
Manage the photos displayed in your website's portfolio/gallery.
* **Add New Photos:**
  1. Use the image uploader to select photos from your computer.
  2. Click **Upload/Save**.
* **Delete Photos:**
  1. Find the photo in your gallery list.
  2. Click the **Delete** icon to remove it.

---

## 📈 Statistics
Update the milestone numbers shown on your homepage (e.g., "10+ Years Experience", "5000+ Happy Trekkers").
* **Update Stats:**
  1. Go to "Statistics" in the sidebar.
  2. Edit the numbers in their respective fields.
  3. Click **Save Changes**.

---

## 📞 Contact Details
Update your phone number, email, address, social media links, office hours, and FAQs.
* **Update Information:**
  1. Change the text in the fields (e.g., Email, Phone, Social Media links).
  2. For Office Hours and FAQs, you can click **Add** to create new ones or the **Delete (Trash)** icon to remove them.
  3. Click the **Save All Changes** button at the bottom of the page.

---

## ⭐ Testimonials
Manage customer reviews shown on the website.
* **Add a Testimonial:**
  1. Fill in the Customer Name, Review Text, and Rating.
  2. Click **Save**.
* **Edit/Delete:**
  1. Find the testimonial in the list.
  2. Click **Edit** to change it, or **Delete** to remove it.

---

## ⚙️ Admin Account
Manage your login credentials.
* **Update Info:** Change your admin email or details.
* **Change Password:** Enter your new password and confirm it.
* **Save:** Ensure you click **Save** so your new login details take effect immediately.

---

**Need Help?** 
If the **🚀 Publish All** button in the sidebar is greyed out with a timer, a deployment is currently in progress. Please wait for the 10-minute countdown to finish before trying to publish new changes again.
